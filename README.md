# senior_design_27-scorpion
Autonomous Ball-Sorting Robot for the 2027 Autonomous Vehicle Competition at ASGC

The Arkansas Space Grant Consortium (ASGC) organizes a statewide challenge where college teams design, build, and program vehicles to navigate a course and complete tasks autonomously. Teams develop their vehicles over two semesters.
For our Senior Design project at the University of Central Arkansas, we’re building Scorpion for the 2027 ASGC Autonomous Vehicle Challenge. In this competition, student teams build robots that can drive through a course and complete tasks on their own.

Scorpion has a 3D-printed chassis, a motorized drivetrain, and a ball intake system. We’ve assembled and driven our third prototype, and we’ve tested the intake. Our next step is to improve the tail’s gear mechanism and bring the mechanical systems, electronics, and software together so Scorpion can complete a full run on its own.
## HOW TO DRIVE SCORPION VIA SSH 

Terminal (Connect to the same network): 
```
ssh scorpion@ip (check raspberry pi ip with "hostname -I")
```
Type in password 

Run:
```
python3 keyboard_drive.py
```
